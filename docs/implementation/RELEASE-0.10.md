# Release 0.10 reviewed Dream feedback acceptance

Scope: feed a selected research-exploration policy into the actual Research
Agent + independent Critic program, compare new complete programs under equal
resource caps, continue research with the admitted policy, record a measured
canary verdict, and return new histories to the next Dream cycle.

## Executed verification

The complete local suite passed **232 tests in 187.21 seconds**. Ruff lint and
format checks passed for 89 Python files; mypy passed for 65 source modules.
The editable package installed as 0.10.0, `pip check` found no broken
requirements, schema export included the new Dream/task contracts, and the
bounded runner CLI exposed its declared limits successfully.

Eighteen new tests cover both complete feedback outcomes, zero-call offline
audit and closed resume, matched-cost enforcement, unknown reservation
retention, concurrent sibling budget races, observed/synthetic scope separation,
forged candidate/finalist rejection, policy stopping audits, receipt-only
recovery versus unknown-call refusal, exposed heldout denial, result-review
budget failure, pause/cancel, finite runner resumption and actual C++ compile
admission accounting. Existing campaign, Iris, Dream and Critic tests also pass.

Initial development attempts exposed a mismatched fixture family, an
incorrectly high budget-failure test cap and a missing test import; these were
corrected before final acceptance. The fixed mathematical evaluator was not
changed. Linux CI includes the complete suite and the new full-loop CLI.

## Executed artificial acceptance

The installed 0.10.0 CLI completed `research dream-loop-fixture` as
**CLOSED / VERIFIED / ROLLED_BACK**. Its final source collected twelve histories
across six mathematical families; development used three families and heldout
used three separate families. It then performed a zero-call catalog screen,
froze one finalist, opened policy-heldout, executed fresh full-program arms and
activated the selected exit-first policy in a new continuous reviewed program.

The changed-condition canary measured a regression. The registry returned to
Q0, and the next continuous program actually ran with Q0. Three new epoch
histories entered a second Dream cycle, which stopped with
`NO_COVERED_DEVELOPMENT_GAIN`; exposed validation data were not reused. A
separate test exercises a non-regressing canary and retained adoption.

The final CLI run recorded:

- Sixteen collection/comparison programs plus two continuous programs.
- Twenty-three sealed epoch histories in total.
- Twenty-three Director, forty-six Critic and two policy-developer role calls.
- Sixty-four artificial backend executions and one declared quota read.
- 6,220 **simulated token units**, with all pending resource dimensions zero.
- Zero external LLM calls, remote Iris submissions and real Hermes studies.

For every fresh/canary arm the same cap covered 64 actions, 8 tool calls,
8,000 token units, 1,000 seconds, 64 compute units and 2 parallel jobs. Each arm
actually completed two Director calls, four Critic calls, a baseline and two
selected interventions; its receipt ledger recorded 520 simulated token units
and three backend tool calls. The mathematical objectives are engineering
controls, not financial results. Provider token/time values are declared fixture
units. Backend local timers and program elapsed time are reported separately;
remote compute, remote queue time and monetary charges remain unavailable.

## Cost, evidence and recovery boundaries

Shared budget ancestry is immutable. Reservations check roles, sibling queues
and ancestor scopes in one write transaction, including unknown pending work.
Full comparisons validate identical task, role/provider configuration, round
limits and complete execution budgets. Compile-only admission, imported
baselines, submit/collect work and policy-development costs remain in the total
study ledger. Corpus acquisition and Dream overhead are reported separately
from application-arm cost; no amortized lifetime efficiency gain is inferred.

Catalog replay remains zero-call screening of recorded independent branches.
It does not predict the full LLM cost or result of a changed adaptive context.
New contexts execute through the reviewed program. Causal decision snapshots,
actual dispatch, Critic outcomes and registry revisions are verified offline.
The registry rejects a different scope/provider protocol or a substituted
finalist. Synthetic evidence cannot authorize observed research.

The bounded `dream-loop-run` runner resumes the same study after RUNNER_LIMIT,
honors pause/cancel and retains unknown-call reservations instead of retrying.
Historical programs without policy/shared-budget fields keep their original
contract. The completed 0.9 real-LLM/artificial-backend Critic program passed the
new offline verifier without a new provider or backend call.

## Limits and next action

This acceptance establishes the engineering control loop and both adoption
outcomes on declared artificial providers. It does not establish general
research efficiency, market profitability, reusable L2 capability learning,
LLM weight updates or unrestricted policy-code generation. Further policy
promotion requires new independent validation tasks after exposure.

Cancelled watchers and the deleted recurring automation remain cancelled.
Private models, data, strategy source and real numerical records stay outside
the public repository. Real Hermes research remains unstarted pending a later
owner task and its frozen baseline/protocol/budget declaration.

Commands and implementation boundaries are recorded in
[P11 reviewed Dream feedback](P11-reviewed-dream-loop.md).

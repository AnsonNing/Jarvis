## Behavior and limits

The persistent study moves from two-day integration to development-period
research. It reuses accepted baselines and creates another finite program when
observed evidence and executable hypotheses justify continuation. By default a
development program permits three direction batches and up to two proposals per
batch; quota and job admission govern actual uploads. There is no eight-run
lifetime cap. Unknown operations retain reservations and stop for reconciliation.

Acknowledgements are snapshots, not an authoritative live remaining-quota
endpoint. The shared ledger applies within the same store/account scope, retains
uncovered attempts and a reserve margin, and cannot observe another user's later
consumption. Missing, stale or exhausted observations wait for newer service
evidence. Changing the local date cannot reset available allowance.

This is L1 strategy research with causal history reuse. It neither retrains LLM
weights nor automatically promotes a research policy. Hermes Dream's L3 engine
remains separately gated by distinct histories, independent tasks, heldout/fresh
comparisons and canary rollback. Repeated experiments on an exposed month do not
meet those independence requirements. L2 capability learning, arbitrary generated
strategy source, complete execution cost/capacity validation and live trading
remain outside this verified release. See [P7](P7-continuous-iris-research.md) for
operating commands and the exact continuation boundaries.
